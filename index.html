Coffee for early shifts and late study nights

Tala started as a coffee cart outside a Mandaluyong review center. Students kept asking us to stay open later, so we found a corner space and did exactly that.

We buy barako and Benguet arabica directly from farms in Batangas and the Cordilleras, roast in small batches, and brew it the way our regulars like it: strong, honest, and fairly priced.

  • Local beans from Batangas and Benguet
  • Free Wi-Fi and outlets at every table
  • Open until 11 PM, every day
